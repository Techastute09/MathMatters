ABOUT THE REGIONAL HUB

Student leadership with community impact.

Math Matters — Uni High Regional Hub is a student-led initiative focused on expanding access to mathematics education through peer tutoring, community engagement, and innovative learning programs.

Our long-term goal is to build a sustainable regional model that can grow with students, tutors, schools, and community partners.
